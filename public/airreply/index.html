AirReply

Talk instead of type. Your words land in whatever app you're using.

Download for Mac

Free · Version 1.2.0 · Requires macOS 26 or later

What it does

  • Click the mic in the menu bar or the floating pill, speak, click again. Your words are pasted where your cursor is.
  • See your words appear live while you talk. Press Esc to throw them away.
  • Works in any app: chat, email, notes, AI assistants.
  • Optionally reads replies aloud, so you can keep the conversation going by voice.
  • Keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, AirReply offers it and installs it with one click.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded AirReply.dmg and drag AirReply into Applications.
  2. Open AirReply from Applications. A mic icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. Allow what it asks for: Microphone (to hear you), Speech Recognition (to type exactly what you said) and Accessibility (to paste into your app).
  4. Right-click the menu-bar icon for Settings and Setup & Permissions.

Privacy

Live words are recognized on your Mac. The final text comes from Apple's speech recognition, the same service Siri and Apple dictation use, so your audio is sent to Apple for that step. Nothing is sent to Custom Fusion.
